Your move

It is your weekly check-in call. You have not hit last week's milestone. An unexpected API rate-limiting issue on the client's infrastructure added 3 days to the integration work. The client's VP of Technology has joined the call unexpectedly. Deliver a verbal status update using the STATUS framework.

What you're practising

Practice using structure to maintain confidence and credibility when the news is mixed. Demonstrate that a setback reported well causes less damage than one buried or minimized.

Debrief focus
  • Did the FDE open with a clear summary rather than a defensive explanation?
  • Was the risk named early and directly?
  • Did they present a path forward?
  • Did the presence of the VP change their composure or their structure?
Each builds on the examples and principles from the content block.
