Section 02 · Email etiquette

Precision over politeness

FDE → Sarah · Tuesday afternoon
A fintech client, mid-engagement. The FDE sends this:
"Hi Sarah, hope you're well! Just wanted to touch base on a few things, firstly the integration timeline, and also the question about the vector database setup we discussed, and also I had a couple of questions about access credentials if you have time. Let me know! Thanks."
Sarah has three direct reports, two meetings that afternoon, and a board prep on Friday. She reads the email, thinks "I'll deal with this later," and moves on. Thursday arrives. None of the items are resolved. The FDE sends a follow-up: "Just following up on my earlier email!" Sarah doesn't reply.
The FDE is now blocked.
Why this happens
In a client-facing role, email is a formal artifact. Every message is a record of how you think, what you prioritize, and how seriously you take the engagement. Vague, multi-threaded emails get deprioritized. Precise, structured ones get answered.
Vague · multi-threaded→deprioritized
Precise · structured→answered
