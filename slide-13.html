The 3 rules of expectation management

01
Early is always better than certain.
Flag the signal, not the certainty.
02
Come with options, not just problems.
Bring the path, not just the problem.
03
Reset explicitly.
Say the change out loud.
2Come with options, not just problems

Raising a risk without a proposed path forward puts the entire burden on the client.

"Here's the situation, here are two ways we can respond, and here's what I recommend" is leadership.

"We have a problem" is not.

The FDE who surfaces issues early, frames them constructively, and comes with options is the one clients trust and renew.
The one who waits until they're certain keeps creating situations like Tuesday.
