Section 03 · Status reporting

Structure is a form of respect

Friday status update · Healthcare AI engagement, Week 6 of 10
The FDE sends:
"This week we continued working on the NLP classification module and made good progress. There were some challenges with the training data quality but we are working through them. Next week we will continue with model fine-tuning and hopefully have something to show soon."
The client's project lead reads this and thinks: what does "good progress" mean? Are we on track? What are the data quality challenges? What does "hopefully" mean for the Week 8 milestone we committed to? She forwards the update to her VP with a note: "I'm not sure where we actually stand."
The beginning of a trust problem
Why structure matters
Status reports are the primary mechanism by which a client stays informed, confident, and aligned with the FDE. A well-written status report does three things simultaneously. A vague one does the opposite of all three.
Proves the FDE is in control Surfaces risks before they become problems Creates a paper trail of accountability
