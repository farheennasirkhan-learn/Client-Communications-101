Scene · Week 5 of 8 — Retail AI project Right fix. Too late.

An FDE notices on Monday that a third-party API the recommendation engine depends on has changed its rate limits. It's fixable, but it will take 4 to 5 days.

"Let me see if I can find a workaround first. No point alarming the client if I can solve it quietly."

By Wednesday, no workaround exists. They tell the client — who had a stakeholder presentation on Tuesday where they told leadership the project was on track.

The client isn't angry about the API change. They're angry about Tuesday.
