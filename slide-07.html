Section 02 · Email etiquette
The framework

The 5 principles of FDE email

01
Lead with the point.
State the purpose of the email in the first line. Clients are busy. They shouldn't need to read three paragraphs to understand what you're asking for or telling them.
02
One email, one ask.
If you need a decision, a review, and a confirmation, send three emails, or structure one very clearly with numbered items. Bundling multiple asks reduces the chance of resolving any of them.
03
Precision over length.
Short and clear beats long and comprehensive. If context is needed, use structure.
04
Tone matches the moment.
Professional warmth is the default. A kickoff email reads differently from a delay notification, which reads differently from a scope conversation. Matching tone to context is a sign of emotional intelligence.
05
Always define the next step.
Every email should end with clarity on what happens next: who does what, by when. If there's no next step, the email probably isn't ready to send.
