Why this matters for an FDE

Three reasons communication sits at the centre of the role — not on the side of it.

Point 01
You are the interface
FDEs operate at the interface between Turing and the client. Every email, every status update, every difficult conversation is a direct representation of Turing's professionalism.
Point 02
Clients are under pressure
Clients in enterprise AI engagements are under pressure. Their leadership is watching. Their timelines are tight. An FDE who communicates with clarity makes their job easier, and that gets noticed.
Point 03
Clarity is how you grow fastest
The FDEs who grow fastest in this role are the ones who make the client feel in control, even when things are difficult. Communication is how that happens.
And it matters more for an FDE than almost any other role, because you're the person the client sees every day.
